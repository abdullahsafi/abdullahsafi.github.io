👋 Hi, I’m Abdullah Safi. Nice to meet you.

I am a Lead Data Scientist at NSW Health specialising in machine learning and advanced analytics. My work involves collaborating with multidisciplinary teams to solve business problems by developing predictive models, analysing complex health data, and building solutions that support statewide initiatives. 

I hold a Bachelor of Advanced Computing (First Class Honours) (Computational Data Science) and a Bachelor of Science (Medical Science) from the [University of Sydney](https://www.sydney.edu.au). Under the supervision of Professor Athman Bouguettaya and the [Sensors, Clouds, and Services Laboratory](http://scslab.net), my thesis project ["Unobtrusive Monitoring for In-home Quality Ageing"](projects/) was produced in collaboration with industry partners [InteliCare](https://intelicare.com.au) and [NSSN](https://www.nssn.org.au).

Outside of work, I enjoy playing chess and basketball, exploring new technologies, and spending quality time with my wife and family.
