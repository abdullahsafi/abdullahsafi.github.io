👋 Hi, I’m Abdullah Safi. Nice to meet you.

I'm currently a Data Scientist at [eHealth NSW](https://www.ehealth.nsw.gov.au). Here, I work on data driven solutions to effectively improve systems at local and state levels across relevant agencies and non government organisations.

I'm a recent graduate of the Bachelor of Advanced Computing (Honours Class 1) and the Bachelor of Science at the [University of Sydney](https://www.sydney.edu.au). My thesis project titled ["Unobtrusive Monitoring for In-home Quality Ageing"](projects/) supervised by Professor Athman Bouguettaya was produced working with industry partners [InteliCare](https://intelicare.com.au) and [NSSN](https://www.nssn.org.au).

My specialisation is in data science and medical science, and I aim to combine the skills from both disciplines and helping people in the community by enhancing health outcomes. 

My research interests include using data to aid the detection of diseases for early diagnosis and personalised medicine. 

My hobbies include chess, basketball and gaming.
